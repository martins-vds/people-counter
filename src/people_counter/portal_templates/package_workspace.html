The app saves each revision automatically in application-managed storage. When generation finishes, download the ZIP to your computer from the completed job or package history.

Revision numbers are assigned automatically. Failed attempts keep their numbers, and existing revisions are never overwritten. Packages contain metadata only; source videos remain unchanged.
